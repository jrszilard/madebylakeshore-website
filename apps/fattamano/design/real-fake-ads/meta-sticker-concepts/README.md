# Real Fake Ads meta sticker concepts

Editable SVG sticker concepts that lean into the meta joke: the buyer pays fattamano to advertise a fake company instead of the company paying the buyer.

These are intentionally different from the first rectangular QR/business-card sticker products. They explore badge, cereal-prize, tombstone, laptop-label, crew-patch, sponsor-label, and yard-sign formats.

Files:

- `real-fake-ads-street-team-badge.svg` — round collection badge: “I paid to advertise a fake company.”
- `oops-all-compliance-cereal-prize.svg` — cereal-box prize sticker for Oops! All Compliance.
- `burialcoin-tombstone-token.svg` — tombstone/token sticker for BurialCoin.
- `model-citizen-ai-laptop-badge.svg` — laptop compliance label for Model Citizen AI.
- `sight-unseen-aerials-ground-crew.svg` — ground-crew/flight patch sticker for Sight Unseen Aerials.
- `samedeck-deck-sponsor-label.svg` — fake sponsorship label for SameDeck Partners.
- `dead-people-yard-sign-mini.svg` — mini yard-sign sticker for Dead People Wanted.
- `preview.html` — quick browser contact sheet for review.

Notes for Wilma:

- All text and shapes are native SVG; no raster art embedded.
- URL copy is included as editable text, not QR. Replace or add a real QR before print if desired.
- Sizes are concept canvases, not final dielines/bleeds.
