# Dead People Wanted Sticker Concepts

Real Fake Ads pilot sticker concepts for the fake funeral home / crematorium at:

`https://fattamano.com/wewantdeadpeople`

## Files

- `sticker-v1-business-card.svg` — strongest recommended direction. Rectangular funeral-home business-card/local-ad sticker, 3.5" × 2.5".
- `sticker-v2-classified-ad.svg` — louder local classifieds / coupon-ad version, 4" × 2".
- `sticker-v3-round-seal.svg` — round crest/seal version, 3" × 3".
- `sticker-v4-burning-skeleton.svg` — funnier mascot version with a polite burning skeleton, 3.5" × 2.5".
- `preview.html` — contact-sheet preview of all four concepts.

## Recommended Direction

Start with **v1 business-card** if the goal is maximum believable-fake-local-ad. Use **v4 burning skeleton** if the goal is funnier/faster read on a sticker while still keeping the funeral-home template look.

## Print Notes

- Replace the QR placeholders with a real scannable QR code before sending to print.
- QR destination: `https://fattamano.com/wewantdeadpeople`
- Keep a small safety cue on the sticker: `A Real Fake Ad • Not a real funeral home`.
- Palette is intentionally matched to the fake website: burgundy, cream, muted gold, dark brown.
- These are SVG mockups; Wilma can redraw/refine in Illustrator/Procreate/Affinity while keeping the layout/copy direction.
